# Last Harvest

**The last farmer. The last chance.**

Last Harvest is a small pixel-style farming strategy game where you're the last farmer left on a dying planet.

You have 30 days to survive. The problem is that the land is running out of life, so every time you harvest something, you're making the land a little worse.

## Play the game

**[Play Last Harvest](https://last-harvest-game.netlify.app/)**

You can also run the game locally by opening `index.html` in a browser.

## How to play

You start with a few plots of land and some resources.

Each day you need food and water to survive. You can plant crops, water them, and harvest them when they're ready.

The catch is that harvesting hurts the land.

You can also rest or use compost to help the land recover. Weather also affects your crops, so you need to pay attention to what's happening each day.

The goal is to make it through all 30 days without letting the land completely die.

### Basic controls

* Click an empty plot to plant something
* Click a growing crop to water it
* Click a ready crop to harvest it
* Use rest and compost when the land needs to recover
* Keep an eye on food, water, and the condition of the land

## What makes it different?

Most farming games are about growing more and getting bigger.

This one is kind of the opposite.

You have to decide when it's actually worth harvesting something because getting more food now can make surviving later harder.

## The idea

I wanted to make a farming game where the main challenge isn't getting more stuff, but deciding what you can afford to use.

Every day gets a little harder, and the choices you make earlier can affect what happens later.
